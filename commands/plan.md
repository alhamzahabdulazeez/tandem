---
name: plan
description: Explore the codebase and propose an approach before writing any code
---

Do not edit anything yet.

1. Read the relevant files. Use search rather than reading whole files.
2. State what you found: the files involved, the current behaviour, the constraint.
3. Propose an approach as a numbered list of steps.
4. Name what could go wrong and what you are uncertain about.
5. Stop and wait for approval.

Keep it under twenty lines. A plan longer than the change is a bad plan.
